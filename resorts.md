### Additional Tips:
- **Transportation**: Most are accessible by private car or bus from terminals like PITX or Cubao in Manila. Traffic can add time, especially on weekends—aim to leave early.
- **Affordability Notes**: These are geared toward budget travelers, with many offering day passes for quick escapes without overnight stays. Food and activities are extra but often inexpensive (e.g., PHP 200-500 for meals).
- **Other Options**: If you're open to slightly less structured beaches, consider public spots like Tali Beach in Nasugbu, Batangas (3 hours drive, minimal entrance fees) or Palm Beach in Laiya, Batangas (3-4 hours, with budget resorts nearby starting around PHP 2,000). For commute-friendly spots, White and Yellow Castle in Lian, Batangas is a simple, affordable choice reachable by bus.
- Book in advance, especially during peak seasons like summer (March-May). Always verify road conditions via apps like Waze.
