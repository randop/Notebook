# Soldering

Video sources for learning soldering, ranging from basic techniques (like equipment setup, flux application, and simple joints) to professional levels (such as surface-mount device soldering, microsoldering, and IPC standards for high-reliability applications). It is organized by table for easy comparison, starting from beginner-friendly and progressing to advanced.

| Source/Platform | Description | Level | Access Notes |
|-----------------|-------------|-------|--------------|
| Adafruit Learning System (learn.adafruit.com) | "Collin's Lab: Soldering" – A beginner video covering basics like why soldering is accessible, common mistakes (e.g., wrong flux types), and simple electronic connections. | Basic | Free video on their site. |
| SparkFun Learn (learn.sparkfun.com) | "How to Solder: Through-Hole Soldering" tutorial includes a dedicated video on soldering headers and basic components to PCBs, with step-by-step demos. | Basic | Free, hosted on Vimeo (non-YouTube embed). |
| Vimeo (vimeo.com) – Tangent Tutorials Series | A series of short videos: #01 on basic equipment; #02 on through-hole techniques; #03 on surface-mount soldering. Practical demos for DIY electronics. | Basic to Intermediate | Free videos: search for "Tangent Tutorial" on Vimeo. |
| Vimeo (vimeo.com) – Miscellaneous Tutorials | Individual videos like "How to Solder" by Steve Johnston (basic techniques for electronics); "Soldering Tutorial Final" by IDMWearables (practical demo); "75 How to Solder Correctly Practical" (hands-on tips). | Basic to Intermediate | Free; search Vimeo for "soldering tutorial" to find these and similar. |
| Ganoksin (ganoksin.com) | "Soldering Techniques from Beginner to Advanced" – Video coaching on rules of soldering, progressing to advanced methods (focused on metals/jewelry but applicable to electronics). | Basic to Advanced | Free video on their site. |
| LinkedIn Learning (linkedin.com/learning) | "Learning Soldering for Electronics" course (2+ hours) – Videos cover setup, tools, basic hand soldering, troubleshooting cold joints, and PCB repairs. | Basic to Intermediate | Requires LinkedIn Premium subscription; includes quizzes. |
| Udemy (udemy.com) – Various Courses | "How to Solder Electronics" (1.5 hours of practical videos on basics); "Pro Soldering Secrets" (tips for perfect joints); "Electronics Soldering Foundation Course" (workspace setup and techniques); "Learning Soldering: Through Hole & Surface Mount Components" (safety, tools, desoldering). | Basic to Intermediate | Paid courses (~$10–20 each, often on sale); self-paced with lifetime access. |
| Udemy (udemy.com) – Advanced Courses | "Introduction to Surface Mount Technology" (10 hours on SMD soldering and circuit design); "Microsoldering: Everything You Need to Know" (4.5 hours curriculum on professional microsoldering for repairs). | Intermediate to Professional | Paid; includes advanced tools and methods. |
| IPC Training Materials (ipc.org or distributors like practicalcomponents.com, soldertools.net) | Professional videos like "Through-Hole Component Prep & Hand Soldering"; "Soldering Iron Tip Care"; wire attachment demos for terminals. Covers IPC standards for high-reliability soldering in industries like aerospace. | Professional | Paid DVDs/downloads or courses (~$50–$200); some previews available; certification-oriented.
