# Pasta

### How to Cook Pasta Perfectly (The Golden Rules)

1. Use a **large pot** with plenty of water (about 4–5 liters for 400–500g pasta).
2. Add a generous amount of **salt** (≈ 1–1.5 tablespoons per 4 liters) — this is your only chance to season the pasta itself!
3. Bring water to a **rolling boil** before adding pasta.
4. Cook according to package time **minus 1–2 minutes** for al dente (firm to the bite).
5. **Reserve 1–2 cups** of starchy pasta water before draining — it's liquid gold for sauce!
6. Never rinse cooked pasta (unless making cold pasta salad).

### Three Easy & Delicious Pasta Dishes

#### 1. Classic Spaghetti Pomodoro (Tomato & Basil) – Fresh & Bright
The ultimate simple Italian favorite.

**Ingredients** (for 2–3 people):
- 300–400g spaghetti
- 4–5 fresh ripe tomatoes (or 1 × 400g good-quality canned San Marzano)
- 3–4 garlic cloves (sliced thin)
- Big handful fresh basil
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Salt + pepper
- Optional: pinch chili flakes

**Quick Steps**:
1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water.
2. In a pan: gently fry garlic in 4–5 tbsp olive oil (don't burn!).
3. Add chopped tomatoes, salt, cook 8–12 min until saucy.
4. Toss drained pasta + splash pasta water in the sauce.
5. Finish with torn basil + extra olive oil.

#### 2. Spaghetti Aglio e Olio (Garlic & Oil) – Fastest 10-Minute Meal
Minimal ingredients, maximum flavor.

**Ingredients**:
- 300–400g spaghetti
- 6–8 garlic cloves (very thinly sliced)
- ½–1 tsp chili flakes (adjust to taste)
- 5–6 tbsp good extra virgin olive oil
- Fresh parsley (optional)
- Grated parmesan or pecorino (optional)

**Steps**:
1. Cook pasta.
2. In a large pan: slowly cook garlic + chili in olive oil until garlic is golden (watch carefully!).
3. Add a big ladle of pasta water + drained pasta.
4. Toss vigorously until creamy emulsion forms.
5. Finish with parsley and cheese if using.

#### 3. Creamy Fettuccine Alfredo – Indulgent Comfort
The rich, cheesy classic.

**Ingredients**:
- 300–400g fettuccine
- 200–250 ml heavy cream
- 100g butter
- 150g freshly grated parmesan
- 1–2 garlic cloves (optional)
- Black pepper + salt
- Optional: chicken strips or shrimp

**Steps**:
1. Cook pasta.
2. In a large pan: melt butter + cream, simmer gently 3–4 min.
3. Add garlic (if using) + tons of black pepper.
4. Toss in drained pasta + most of the cheese + pasta water until silky.
5. Finish with extra cheese on top.
