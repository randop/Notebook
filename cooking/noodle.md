# Noodle

## Handmade Wheat Noodles

**Ingredients** (makes 2–3 servings):
- 2 cups (about 250g) all-purpose or bread flour
- ½ tsp salt
- ~½ cup (115g) water (adjust slightly for a firm dough)

**Steps**:
1. Mix flour and salt. Slowly add water while stirring until it forms a rough dough.
2. Knead 3–5 minutes until smooth (it'll be firm/stiff—that's normal for chewy noodles).
3. Cover and rest 30 minutes (or up to 2 hours for easier rolling).
4. Divide in half. On a floured surface, roll each piece very thin (aim for 1–2 mm).
5. Lightly flour, fold loosely, and slice into strips. Unfold and dust with a bit more flour or cornmeal to prevent sticking.
6. Boil 2–4 minutes in salted water (taste for doneness—they should be chewy, not soft).
7. Rinse briefly under cold water if using in a cold dish, or toss directly into soup/stir-fry.

## Recipes

### 1. Pad Thai (Thai Stir-Fried Rice Noodles)
The ultimate crowd-pleaser: tangy, sweet, nutty, and a little spicy. It's one of the most iconic noodle dishes ever.

**Ingredients** (serves 2–3):
- 200g rice noodles (flat, medium width)
- 200g chicken, shrimp, or tofu (sliced)
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
- 100g bean sprouts
- 2–3 green onions, sliced
- 3 Tbsp tamarind paste (or lime juice + brown sugar mix)
- 3 Tbsp fish sauce (or soy sauce for vegetarian)
- 2 Tbsp palm sugar or brown sugar
- 1–2 Tbsp chili flakes or sauce (adjust to taste)
- 3 Tbsp oil
- Crushed peanuts, lime wedges, cilantro for topping

**Steps**:
1. Soak rice noodles in warm water 10–15 minutes until soft; drain.
2. Mix sauce: tamarind, fish sauce, sugar, chili.
3. Heat oil in a wok; stir-fry garlic 30 seconds.
4. Add protein; cook until done. Push aside, scramble eggs.
5. Add noodles + sauce; toss 2–3 minutes until coated and noodles soften.
6. Throw in bean sprouts + green onions; stir-fry 1 minute.
7. Serve topped with peanuts, lime squeeze, and cilantro.

### 2. Homemade Ramen (Rich Broth Bowl)
Comfort in a bowl—customizable with toppings. This version uses a quick flavorful broth.

**Ingredients** (serves 2):
- 2 packs fresh or instant ramen noodles (discard seasoning if using instant)
- 4 cups chicken or veggie broth
- 2 Tbsp miso paste
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 garlic cloves + 1-inch ginger, minced
- Toppings: soft-boiled eggs, sliced green onions, nori, corn, mushrooms, chashu pork or tofu, chili oil

**Steps**:
1. Simmer broth with garlic, ginger, miso, soy, and sesame oil 10–15 minutes.
2. Boil noodles separately per package (usually 2–3 minutes); drain.
3. Divide noodles into bowls; ladle hot broth over.
4. Add toppings generously—soft egg is key!

### 3. Lo Mein (Chinese Stir-Fried Noodles)
Classic takeout-style: saucy, chewy, loaded with veggies and protein. Super quick!

**Ingredients** (serves 3–4):
- 300g fresh egg noodles or lo mein noodles (or spaghetti in a pinch)
- 200g chicken, beef, pork, or tofu (sliced thin)
- 2 cups mixed veggies (broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, cabbage)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- Sauce: 3 Tbsp soy sauce, 2 Tbsp oyster sauce (or hoisin for veg), 1 Tbsp sesame oil, 1 tsp sugar, 1 Tbsp cornstarch + ¼ cup water
- 2–3 Tbsp oil

**Steps**:
1. Boil noodles until just tender; drain and rinse briefly.
2. Mix sauce ingredients.
3. Heat oil in wok; stir-fry protein until browned; remove.
4. Stir-fry garlic + veggies 2–3 minutes.
5. Add noodles + protein back; pour sauce over and toss 2 minutes until glossy and coated.
6. Serve hot—add chili oil if you like heat!
