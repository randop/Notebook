# Filipino-style burrito

## Filipino Breakfast Burrito

**Serves:** 4  
**Prep/Cook:** ~20-30 minutes

#### Ingredients:
- **4 large flour tortillas** (burrito size)
- **2½ cups cooked rice** (day-old is best for sinangag)
- **225-300g tocino or longganisa** (or leftover chicken/pork adobo, shredded)
- **3-4 eggs**
- **1-2 tbsp butter or cooking oil**
- **Garlic** (minced, 4-6 cloves for rice)
- **1-1½ cups shredded lettuce or cabbage**
- **2-3 tomatoes**, diced
- **½-1 cup shredded cheddar or quickmelt cheese** (or kesong puti)
- **Optional add-ins**: Banana ketchup or atchara (pickled papaya) for tang, sliced onions, chili flakes or labuyo for heat, avocado if available, sour cream (or mayo-lemon mix)

#### Quick Steps:
1. **Cook the meat**: Fry tocino or longganisa in a pan until caramelized and cooked through (add a bit of water first if using tocino, then let it fry in its own fat). Slice or crumble. For adobo, shred leftover meat and warm with some sauce. Set aside.

2. **Garlic fried rice (Sinangag)**: In the same pan, sauté minced garlic in oil until golden and fragrant. Add cooked rice, season with salt (and a splash of soy sauce or vinegar for extra flavor). Stir-fry until hot and slightly crispy. Optional: Mix in a bit of banana ketchup for sweetness.

3. **Scrambled eggs**: Beat eggs with a splash of milk (optional). Melt butter in a pan, pour in eggs, and stir gently until just set. Add some cheese toward the end so it melts in.

4. **Assemble**:
   - Warm tortillas in a dry pan or microwave (10-20 seconds) for flexibility.
   - Lay tortilla flat. Spread a layer of garlic rice down the center.
   - Add meat, scrambled eggs, shredded lettuce/cabbage, diced tomatoes, extra cheese.
   - Drizzle with banana ketchup, hot sauce, or a simple mayo-sriracha mix.
   - Fold: Tuck in sides, then roll tightly from the bottom.

5. **Optional sear**: Place the wrapped burrito seam-side down in a hot pan for 1-2 minutes per side to crisp it up and seal.
