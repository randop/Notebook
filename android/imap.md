# IMAP

### Android email apps with IMAP support

1. **Gmail** – Google's default, decent third-party IMAP support
2. **FairEmail** – open source, on-device processing, unlimited IMAP accounts
3. **K-9 Mail / Thunderbird for Android** – open source, being merged into Mozilla's Thunderbird brand
4. **BlueMail** – cross-platform, unified inbox, IMAP/POP3/Exchange
5. **Outlook** – Microsoft, strong for 365/Exchange but handles IMAP too
6. **Spark Mail** – AI features, smart inbox, IMAP support
7. **Edison Mail** – free tier, package tracking, smart features
8. **Aqua Mail** – highly configurable, long-time Android IMAP client
9. **Samsung Email** – preloaded on Samsung devices, full IMAP support
10. **Yahoo Mail** – native plus generic IMAP account support
11. **myMail** (Mail.ru) – unified inbox across providers
12. **TypeApp Email** – customizable UI, multi-account IMAP
13. **Nine** – Exchange-focused but also does IMAP/POP3
14. **R2Mail2** – business-oriented, S/MIME, IMAP IDLE support
15. **MailDroid** – older but still maintained IMAP/POP3 client
16. **Email App by MobiSystems** – general-purpose IMAP client
17. **CloudMagic-style "Newton"** style clients — largely discontinued, worth skipping
18. **Inbox by Yandex** – if you use Yandex mail, decent IMAP support
19. **Fastmail app** – IMAP under the hood, tied to Fastmail accounts
20. **K9 Mail forks** (e.g. on F-Droid) – if you want something closer to your self-hosted vibemail setup for testing
