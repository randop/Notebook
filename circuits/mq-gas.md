# MQ series gas sensors 

The **MQ series** gas sensors (from Winsen and similar manufacturers) are low-cost, semiconductor-based (SnO₂) sensors commonly used with microcontrollers like Arduino for detecting various gases. They operate at ~5V, have analog output (voltage changes with gas concentration), and often a digital output (threshold via potentiometer). They require pre-heating (often 24–48 hours initially for stability) and are sensitive to environmental factors like temperature/humidity.

- **MQ2**: Smoke, LPG (liquefied petroleum gas), methane (CH₄), butane, propane, hydrogen (H₂), carbon monoxide (CO). Versatile for flammable/smoke detection.
- **MQ3**: Alcohol (ethanol), benzene, smoke, LPG, CO, hexane, methane. Best known for breathalyzer/alcohol vapor detection.
- **MQ4**: Methane (CH₄), compressed natural gas (CNG). High sensitivity to methane.
- **MQ5**: Natural gas (mostly methane), LPG, town gas. Good for household/industrial gas leak detection.
- **MQ6**: LPG, butane, propane. Focused on LPG detection with some sensitivity to other flammables like methane, alcohol, hydrogen, smoke.
- **MQ7**: Carbon monoxide (CO). Specialized for CO detection (often with heater cycling for better accuracy).
- **MQ8**: Hydrogen (H₂). High sensitivity to hydrogen gas (100–10,000 ppm range), with good rejection of other gases.
- **MQ9**: Carbon monoxide (CO), flammable gases (e.g., methane, LPG). Similar to MQ7 but broader flammable detection.
- **MQ135**: Air quality sensor — ammonia (NH₃), benzene, alcohol, smoke, CO₂, NOx, sulfides, other harmful/toxic vapors. "All-rounder" for indoor air quality monitoring.
