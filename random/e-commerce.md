# E-Commerce

### **Shopee vs Lazada in the Philippines: Key Metrics (2024-2025 Data)**

**Shopee** dominates the Philippine e-commerce market across nearly all major indicators, holding a clear lead over **Lazada**. While Lazada remains a strong second-place player (backed by Alibaba), Shopee (owned by Sea Limited) has pulled ahead in market share, traffic, and sales volume. Emerging competitors like TikTok Shop are gaining ground but haven't overtaken the top two yet.

### Hard Facts & Insights
- **Dominance in Traffic & Usage**: Shopee consistently captures over half of e-commerce traffic and visits in the Philippines, roughly **2x Lazada's share**. This is driven by aggressive promotions, gamification (e.g., vouchers, coins), and mobile-first features.
- **GMV & Sales Leadership**: Shopee's higher GMV share translates to significantly more transaction volume. In April 2024 alone, Shopee outperformed Lazada by nearly 80% in monthly revenue on the platforms.
- **Growth Trends**: The Philippine e-commerce market is booming (projected ~USD 17-18 billion in 2025), with mobile accounting for ~79% of GMV. Shopee benefits more from this due to higher app engagement.
- **Challenges for Lazada**: After restructuring in 2024, Lazada stabilized regionally and achieved profitability, focusing on premium brands and AI logistics. However, it has lost ground to Shopee in value-driven segments.
- **Rising Threat**: TikTok Shop is growing fast (e.g., strong in live commerce), but as of 2024, Shopee and Lazada still control the bulk of the market.

Overall, **Shopee is the clear winner** in the Philippines for scale and consumer preference, backed by these quantifiable metrics from reliable sources like Statista, Momentum Works, TMO Group, and Similarweb. If you're a seller or shopper prioritizing reach and deals, Shopee edges out. Lazada excels in logistics reliability and branded products.
