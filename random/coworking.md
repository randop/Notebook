# Coworking

## Coworking spaces in **Cubao**, Quezon City, Metro Manila, Philippines

### 1. Regus (Gateway Tower) – Professional & Flexible Coworking
- **Address**: 5/F Gateway Tower, Araneta Center, Cubao, Quezon City (steps from Gateway Mall and Araneta Coliseum).
- **Pricing**: Day coworking from ₱950/person; dedicated desks from ₱220/day (on longer contracts); Coworking Access Plan ~₱300/day (unlimited monthly access).
- **Highlights**: Fully serviced with high-speed WiFi, ergonomic desks, meeting rooms, printing, and global network access (use other Regus/Spaces locations worldwide). Drop-in or book via app. Great for freelancers, teams, or business travelers.


### 2. Racket Room Collective – Community & Creative Vibe
- **Address**: 94 10th Avenue (Unit 3G, Symphony Tower 1), Cubao, Quezon City.
- **Hours**: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–9 PM (events on weekends).
- **Pricing**: Monthly from ~₱6,000; daily/weekly packages available (check site for exacts).
- **Highlights**: Shared desks, meeting/event spaces, high-speed WiFi, air-con, lounge areas, and a relaxed community feel (often used for workshops and networking). Contact: (02) 8282-3736, 0908-881-6721, or racketroom@gmail.com.

### 3. strEATs Coworking Cafe (ibis Styles Manila Araneta City)
- **Address**: Level 4, ibis Styles Manila, Gateway Mall 2, General Roxas Avenue, Araneta City, Cubao.
- **Hours**: Open 24/7 (hotel guests); walk-ins typically 6 AM–10 PM.
- **Pricing**: ₱500 for 3 hours (includes brewed coffee + cookie + water/WiFi); ₱900 for 6 hours (includes sandwich + soda); group packages available.
- **Highlights**: Hotel cafe setting with comfy seating, long counters, power outlets, fast WiFi, and grab-and-go food/snacks. Hotel security and mall/train access.

### 4. SolAlto Studios Cubao Creative Coworking Space
- **Address**: Regalia Park Towers, 150 P. Tuazon Blvd., Cubao, Quezon City.
- **Pricing**: ~₱75/hour or ₱250 for 4 hours (soft-opening rates; confirm current).
- **Highlights**: Creative-focused hub for artists, freelancers, and innovators with collaborative workspace. By appointment only (via Facebook Messenger).

**Tips**: 
- Gateway/Araneta area (Regus + strEATs) is the most accessible by public transport.
- Book ahead, especially for peak hours or meetings.
- Many offer free trials or day passes—perfect for testing.
- For more options nearby (e.g., in broader Quezon City), apps like Coworker.com or Google Maps list extras.
