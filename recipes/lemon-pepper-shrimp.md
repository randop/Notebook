# Lemon Pepper Shrimp

A fast, bright skillet recipe that lets the lemon pepper shine. Ready in about 15 minutes.

### Ingredients (serves 3–4)
- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined (tails on or off)
- 1½–2 tablespoons lemon pepper powder
- 2 tablespoons olive oil or butter
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- Juice of 1 lemon (plus extra wedges for serving)
- ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional, for heat)
- Salt to taste (only if your lemon pepper blend is low-sodium)
- Fresh parsley or chives, chopped
- Optional: 2 tablespoons white wine or chicken broth for a light sauce

### Instructions
1. Pat the shrimp very dry with paper towels. Toss them in a bowl with the lemon pepper powder until evenly coated.
2. Heat the olive oil or butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes (if using) and cook 30–45 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add the seasoned shrimp in a single layer. Cook 1–2 minutes per side until pink and opaque (don’t overcook or they’ll turn rubbery).
4. Squeeze the lemon juice over the shrimp. If you want a light sauce, pour in the wine or broth and let it bubble for 30–60 seconds, scraping up any browned bits.
5. Taste and adjust seasoning. Sprinkle with fresh herbs and serve immediately with lemon wedges.

### Serving ideas
- Over rice, quinoa, or pasta
- With crusty bread to soak up the juices
- Alongside roasted asparagus, broccoli, or a simple salad
- Stuffed into tacos or lettuce wraps with avocado

**Quick tip:** For extra flavor, marinate the shrimp in a little olive oil + lemon pepper for 10–15 minutes before cooking.
