# Lemon Pepper Chicken

A quick, flavorful weeknight recipe featuring juicy chicken coated in bright, zesty lemon pepper seasoning.

### Ingredients (serves 4)
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (or thighs), about 1½–2 lbs total  
- 2–3 tablespoons lemon pepper powder (adjust to taste)  
- 2 tablespoons olive oil (or melted butter)  
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder  
- ½ teaspoon salt (optional — many lemon pepper blends already contain salt)  
- Juice of ½ fresh lemon (optional, for extra brightness)  
- Fresh parsley or lemon wedges for serving (optional)

### Instructions
1. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels. This helps the seasoning stick and promotes better browning.
2. In a small bowl, mix the lemon pepper powder, garlic powder, and salt (if using).
3. Rub the chicken all over with olive oil, then generously coat both sides with the lemon pepper mixture. Let it sit for 10–15 minutes at room temperature (or up to 30 minutes in the fridge) if you have time.
4. **Pan-sear method (quickest):** Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add a drizzle of oil if needed. Cook the chicken 5–7 minutes per side (depending on thickness) until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C) and the outside is golden. In the last minute, squeeze the fresh lemon juice over the top if desired.
5. **Oven method:** Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Place seasoned chicken on a lightly oiled baking sheet or in a baking dish. Bake 20–25 minutes (or until 165°F), flipping halfway if desired. Broil for the last 1–2 minutes for extra crispiness.
6. Rest the chicken 3–5 minutes before slicing. Sprinkle with fresh parsley and serve with lemon wedges.

### Serving suggestions
- Pair with roasted vegetables, rice, mashed potatoes, a simple green salad, or pasta tossed in olive oil and garlic.
- For a creamier twist, make a quick pan sauce: after cooking, deglaze the skillet with a splash of chicken broth or white wine, add a pat of butter, and a squeeze of lemon.

**Tips**  
- Thicker chicken breasts cook more evenly if you pound them to even thickness or butterfly them.  
- Taste your lemon pepper powder first — some blends are saltier or more peppery than others.  
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for 3–4 days and are great sliced over salads or in wraps.
