# Keyboard

## Mechanical Keyboard Switches

### Classification by Actuation Type

**Linear** — Smooth keystroke from top to bottom with no tactile bump or audible click. Preferred for gaming due to fast, uninterrupted actuation.

**Tactile** — A palpable bump partway through the keystroke signals actuation without an accompanying click sound. Common for typing.

**Clicky** — Combines a tactile bump with an audible click, produced by a secondary internal component (click bar or click jacket).

### Specialty and Modern Variants

**Holy Panda** — Hybrid switch combining a Halo True stem with an Invyr/Gateron housing, producing a sharp, rounded tactile bump. Community-developed, not from a single original manufacturer.

**Silent Red / Silent Black** — Linear switches with dampening pads on the stem to reduce bottom-out and top-out noise.

**Alpaca** — Linear switch with factory lubrication and a long pole stem, designed to reduce wobble.

**Boba U4** — Tactile switch with a rounded, pronounced bump, designed by ThereminGoat.

**Zealios** — Tactile switch line by ZealPC, known for a strong, defined tactile bump, available in multiple weights (62g, 65g, 67g, 78g).

---

### Housing and Stem Materials

- **PC (Polycarbonate)** — Common for top housings, allows light transmission for RGB.
- **Nylon (PA66)** — Used for bottom housings, produces a deeper sound profile.
- **POM (Polyoxymethylene)** — Used for stems, self-lubricating and low-friction.
